Founder Note

Hello! I'm George Miako, a young Starehe alumnus (File No. 15579, Pele House, Class of 2023) and a third-year Mechatronic Engineering student at JKUAT.

Beyond engineering, I’ve developed a strong interest in photography, filmmaking, and visual storytelling. Over the years, I’ve spent a lot of time experimenting with composition, lighting, editing, and cinematic aesthetics through personal projects and recreational photography.

Frames By Miako is my attempt to turn that interest into a serious creative venture while I still have the freedom, energy, and time for ambitious experiments.

Combining engineering discipline, precision lighting geometry, and visual aesthetics to craft high-impact commercial media.

The Horizon

What I Want to Achieve

Frames By Miako is an early-stage photography and film venture currently in its launch phase. The goal is to serve the hospitality, real estate, automotive, and corporate sectors through photography and cinematic videography.

This will be achieved by specializing in hotel features, architectural showcases, luxury home tours, automotive content, and promotional films designed to help businesses stand out in a highly competitive digital landscape.

Curated hotel features, resort visuals, restaurant interiors, and guest experience cinematic shorts.

The Community Pillar

Supporting Frames by Miako

Frames by Miako is currently in its launch phase and is being built through personal investment, continuous learning, and the support of people who believe in encouraging ambitious young people to pursue meaningful ventures.

If this vision resonates with you, I invite you to be part of its early story by contributing toward the purchase of the production equipment that will form the foundation of the studio.

Every contribution, no matter the size, goes directly toward tangible production assets that expand the venture’s capabilities and accelerate its transition from personal projects to professional work.

While financial contributions are invaluable, support also extends beyond funding. Advice, mentorship, introductions, referrals and opportunities to collaborate are equally appreciated and can be just as impactful in helping a young venture grow.

I am deeply grateful to everyone who chooses to invest their confidence, encouragement or resources in this journey.
